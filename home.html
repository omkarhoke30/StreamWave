StreamWave is a movie streaming platform UI project where users can explore movies, watch trailers, and browse different categories.

No, you can browse movies and watch trailers without creating an account. However, signing in allows access to personalized features.

Is StreamWave free to use???

Yes, this platform is completely free and designed for learning and demonstration purposes

Is this an official streaming platform??

No, this is a frontend project for educational purposes only and is not affiliated with any real streaming service.

Can I watch full movies on StreamWave??

No, StreamWave provides trailers only, not full movies.

Who developed StreamWave??

StreamWave is developed as a personal project to showcase web development skills.
